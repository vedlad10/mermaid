The problem

A claim sets an expectation. The payment says what actually happened.

The difference is where the money goes, and in most practices nobody owns it. The front desk checked the benefits, billing sent the claim, the insurer paid less, and the gap sits unread in a remittance file.

  • Below-contract payments
  • Denials for missing documents
  • Downcoded procedures
  • Coordination of benefits

Our principles

How we keep ourselves honest

  • Every figure has a source. Each number traces back to the insurance check or payment that produced it.
  • A person approves anything that leaves the practice. Recover drafts; your team decides and sends.
  • Examples are labelled as examples. The numbers on this page are illustrative. On a call, we'll look at yours.
  • No clinical judgement. Recover works on the money side only: coverage, claims, payments and balances.

Security

Built for patient data

  • BAA first. We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any patient data is shared.
  • Encrypted patient details. Patient fields are encrypted in the database, with the key held outside the code.
  • Walled-off data. Each practice's records are isolated at the database level.
  • A full trail. Every change is recorded as an event, so nothing is edited silently.

FAQ

Questions practices ask us

Something else on your mind? Email us and a founder will reply.

Does Recover replace my practice management software?

No. Recover works alongside it. It reads the insurer's payment files and a claims export from your practice software, and shows where the two disagree.

Which practice software do you work with?

Recover reads the standard insurer payment file (the 835/ERA) that every clearinghouse provides. For the claims side we have a step-by-step export guide for Open Dental, and we'll walk you through exports from other systems on the call.

Will Recover contact insurers or patients for us?

No. Recover drafts appeals and ranks what to chase. Your team reviews, approves and sends. Nothing leaves your practice without a person signing off.

How is patient data protected?

We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any patient data is shared. Patient fields are encrypted in the database, each practice's records are isolated, and every change is recorded.
