How it works

Mermaid takes the insurance check, the claim and the insurer's payment file (the 835/ERA), and keeps them in one record. Every figure traces back to the payment that produced it.

  1. 01

    Expected

    What the plan and your contract say each line should pay.

  2. 02

    Actual

    What the insurer's payment file says it paid.

  3. 03

    The gap

    The difference, procedure by procedure.

  4. 04

    The reason

    Denial, limit, rate or missing document, named.

  5. 05

    The recovery

    A queue ranked by dollars, with an action on every line.

How we keep ourselves honest

  • Every figure has a source. Each number traces back to the insurance check or payment that produced it.
  • A person approves anything that leaves the practice. Mermaid drafts; your team decides and sends.
  • Examples are labelled as examples. The numbers on this page are illustrative. On a call, we'll look at yours.
  • No clinical judgement. Mermaid works on the money side only: coverage, claims, payments and balances.

Built for patient data

  • BAA first. We sign a Business Associate Agreement before any patient data is shared.
  • Encrypted patient details. Patient fields are encrypted in the database, with the key held outside the code.
  • Walled-off data. Each practice's records are isolated at the database level.
  • A full trail. Every change is recorded as an event, so nothing is edited silently.
